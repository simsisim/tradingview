In the 2019 Advance Bootcamp, a member presented the idea of profiting from Analyst Upgrades and Downgrades. He used it primarily for day trading. I developed the setup idea of using analyst price target changes against just upgrades and downgrades to trade stocks from that idea. That became the ANA3 setup, which I trade now.  

In 2015 a Stockbee member developed a Days Above Moving Average DAMA as a way to isolate persistent stocks and rank them and trade minor pullbacks on it. I refined that idea to create a yearly Persistent momentum ranking.

Ideas are everywhere; if you participate in social learning, you can pick up many good ideas and build setups around them.
